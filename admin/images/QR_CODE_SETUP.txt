========================================
 HOW TO ADD YOUR GPay QR CODE
========================================

Step 1: Get Your QR Code
-------------------------
1. Open your Google Pay (GPay) app
2. Go to your profile/business account
3. Click on "QR Code" or "Your QR Code"
4. Download or take a screenshot of your QR code
5. Save it as an image file (PNG or JPG)

Step 2: Add QR Code to Website
-------------------------------
1. Rename your QR code image to: gpay-qr-code.png
2. Copy the image to this folder:
   Men-Salon-Management-System-Project-PHP\msms\images\

Step 3: Test It
---------------
1. Go to: http://localhost/Men-Salon-Management-System-Project-PHP/msms/checkout.php
2. Add products to cart
3. Select "GPay / UPI Payment"
4. Click "Place Order"
5. You will see your QR code in the popup!

Alternative Options:
--------------------
- You can use PhonePe, Paytm, or any UPI QR code
- Just save it as gpay-qr-code.png and add to images folder
- Make sure the image is clear and scannable
- Recommended size: 500x500 pixels or larger

Important Notes:
----------------
- Keep your QR code image secure
- Only share it on your official website
- Regularly verify that the QR code is working
- Test with a small amount first
